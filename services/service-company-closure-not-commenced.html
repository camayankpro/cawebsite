COMPANY CLOSURE

Closure of a Company That Has Not Commenced Business

Agarwal Mayank & Company — Chartered Accountants Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, India

If your private limited company was incorporated but never started business, keeping it alive still costs money. Annual compliance, audit and filing obligations continue even when there is no activity. Striking the company off the register is often the cleaner option. We handle the full process with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) on your behalf.

When This Service Applies

A company can usually apply for strike-off under Section 248 of the Companies Act, 2013, where it has not commenced business. Typical conditions are:

  • The company was incorporated but has not started any business activity since incorporation
  • It has no assets, liabilities or pending dues to creditors, employees, government or any other party
  • It has no pending legal proceedings against it
  • The directors and shareholders agree to the closure

Important: If the company has missed filings (for example, annual returns or financial statements), the route and the steps change. A company with unfiled forms may first need to bring its filings up to date. We check this before recommending a route, so you know what applies to your company.

Our Process, Step by Step

Step 1: Eligibility check. We review the incorporation documents, the MCA master data, and the company's filing history. This tells us whether a direct strike-off is possible.

Step 2: Clearing position. We confirm that there are no liabilities or pending dues, and advise on any filings that must be completed first.

Step 3: Board and shareholder approvals. We prepare the board resolution and the shareholder consents required for the application.

Step 4: Declarations and application. We prepare the required declarations from the directors and file the application with the ROC through the MCA portal.

Step 5: ROC notice and objection period. The ROC publishes a public notice and allows a period for objections. We monitor the portal and respond to any queries from the ROC.

Step 6: Removal from the register. If no valid objection is raised, the ROC removes the company's name from the register. We confirm the outcome and hand over the final documents.

Documents Usually Required

  • Certificate of incorporation, PAN and TAN of the company
  • Digital signature certificates (DSC) of the directors
  • Director and shareholder details, with identity and address proof
  • Bank details of the company, with a statement showing no transactions or a closing confirmation
  • Declaration that the company has not commenced business and has no liabilities

Timeline

The total time depends on the ROC's processing and the objection period. Once the application is filed, the process usually takes several months. We give you a realistic timeline after the eligibility check, based on your company's situation.

Common Reasons Closures Get Delayed

  • Unfiled annual returns or financial statements
  • A bank account with unresolved transactions
  • GST registration still active, which must be cancelled separately
  • Disagreement between directors or shareholders about the closure
  • Missing or expired DSC of a director

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a company be closed if it has never done any business?

Yes, in most cases. A company that has not commenced business can usually apply for strike-off, provided it has no liabilities and meets the other conditions. The eligibility check confirms this for your company.

Do I need to file pending annual returns first?

It depends on your company's filing history. If returns are pending, they may need to be filed before or as part of the closure. We confirm this during the eligibility check.

Will the directors be personally liable after closure?

Directors may remain responsible for any liabilities or wrongful statements made in the application. Accurate declarations are important, which is why we check the company's position before filing.

What if the company has a GST number?

GST registration is separate from company strike-off. It must be cancelled with the GST department, and we can handle that as part of the same engagement.

Can this be done from anywhere in India?

Yes. The process is handled online through the MCA portal. Clients across India can engage us remotely, and we are based in Hapur, Uttar Pradesh.
