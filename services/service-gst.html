Why Timely GST Compliance Matters

  • Late filing attracts a daily penalty, subject to a maximum cap. The rates and caps are set out in the return-specific pages.
  • Interest applies on delayed tax payment.
  • GSTIN can be suspended or cancelled for non-filing.
  • Buyers lose ITC on purchases from non-compliant suppliers.
  • Proper ITC reconciliation can reduce your tax outgo.

Who Needs Our GST Services?

Any business with mandatory or voluntary GST registration, including traders, manufacturers, service providers, e-commerce sellers, exporters and startups, needs regular GST compliance support.

How Our GST Service Works

1. Understand your business. We review your registration, turnover, supply types and filing frequency, so the services you need are set up correctly from the start.

2. Set up the compliance calendar. We confirm the due dates for your returns, e-way bills and LUT, and track them through the year.

3. Monthly cycle. We reconcile sales and purchases, prepare the returns and file them, and share a summary for your approval before each filing.

4. Issues and notices. If a notice arrives, we review it, reply within the deadline and follow up with the department.

5. Year-end. We support the annual return, any reconciliation statement required, and an LUT renewal for the new financial year.

Who We Work With

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need all these services?

Not usually. Most businesses need returns and reconciliation every month, and the others only when a specific event happens, such as an export, a notice or a movement of goods. We recommend only what applies to you.

Can you handle GST for more than one business?

Yes. We manage multiple GSTINs and keep each one separate, with its own calendar and filings.

Do you work with clients outside Hapur?

Yes. GST filing is done online, so we serve clients across India. Our office is in Hapur, Uttar Pradesh.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the number of GSTINs, the filing frequency, the volume of invoices and the services you need. We give a clear quote after a short review of your business.
