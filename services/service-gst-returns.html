GST Return Filing: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-9

Agarwal Mayank & Company — Chartered Accountants Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, India

Every GST-registered business must file returns on time, even when there were no sales in the period. Returns report your outward supplies, your tax liability and the input tax credit you claim. Errors in one return flow into the next, so accurate monthly or quarterly filing protects your credit and avoids notices.

Which Returns Apply to Your Business

  • GSTR-1: details of outward supplies (sales) made in the period
  • GSTR-3B: summary return with tax payable and ITC claimed, filed monthly or quarterly
  • GSTR-9: annual return, for taxpayers who need to file one
  • GSTR-9C: reconciliation statement, where an audit is required

Monthly or quarterly? Businesses under the QRMP scheme with turnover up to the prescribed limit may file quarterly, with a monthly payment of tax. We check your turnover and the scheme each year, so you are on the right filing frequency.

Our Filing Process

Step 1: Data collection. We collect sales registers, purchase invoices, credit and debit notes, and e-invoice data.

Step 2: Reconciliation. We match your sales with the GSTR-1 data and your purchases with GSTR-2B, and flag mismatches before filing.

Step 3: Preparation. We prepare the return, check the tax calculation and the ITC claimed, and share a summary for your approval.

Step 4: Filing and payment. We file the return and make the tax payment from the cash ledger, then send you the acknowledgement.

Due Dates

  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B: due dates fall on set days of the following month, depending on your filing frequency
  • GSTR-9: annual return, with a due date at the end of December after the financial year

Important: Due dates change from time to time through notifications. We confirm the current dates for your filing frequency before each period.

What Happens When Returns Are Late

  • A late fee applies for each day of delay, with separate rates for nil returns and returns with tax payable, subject to a maximum cap
  • Interest applies on tax paid late
  • Repeated non-filing can lead to suspension or cancellation of your GSTIN
  • Your buyers may lose ITC on invoices you have not reported

Documents We Need Each Period

  • Sales register and the list of invoices issued
  • Purchase register and supplier invoices
  • Credit notes and debit notes issued or received
  • Details of exempt, nil-rated and export supplies
  • Bank details for the tax payment

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to file a return if there were no sales?

Yes. A nil return must be filed for each period in which the business is registered, unless an exemption applies. We confirm this for your registration.

What is the difference between GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?

GSTR-1 reports your sales in detail. GSTR-3B is the summary return where you pay tax and claim input tax credit.

Why does my ITC not match GSTR-2B?

Differences usually come from suppliers not filing on time, wrong GSTIN, or invoices recorded in different periods. We reconcile these before filing.

Can you file returns for past missed periods?

Yes. We review the pending periods, file them in order, and calculate the late fees and interest payable.

Can this be done from anywhere in India?
