Change of Partner in LLP

Agarwal Mayank & Company — Chartered Accountants Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, India

When a partner joins, leaves or changes role in a Limited Liability Partnership, the LLP must update its records with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. A delay can create penalties and make the LLP's records inaccurate, which affects bank, tax and loan dealings.

What Changes Need Filing

  • A new partner is added
  • An existing partner resigns or is removed
  • A partner's contribution, address or identity details change
  • A partner is appointed or removed as designated partner

Important: The filing is generally due within 30 days of the change. Late filing attracts additional fees, so please contact us as soon as a change takes place.

Our Process, Step by Step

Step 1: Review the change. We confirm the date of the change, the partners involved and the supporting documents.

Step 2: Consents and agreements. We prepare the partner's consent, the resignation letter or the appointment record, and the updated LLP agreement if needed.

Step 3: Digital signatures. We confirm that the partners' digital signature certificates are valid and arrange new ones where required.

Step 4: Filing. We prepare and file the change in the required MCA form, with any associated fees.

Step 5: Confirmation. We track the filing through to the MCA update and send you the confirmation.

Documents Usually Required

  • LLP identification number (LLPIN) and the LLP agreement
  • Identity and address proof of each new partner, with consent
  • Resignation letter or removal record for any outgoing partner
  • Valid digital signature certificates of the partners

Timeline

Once the documents are ready, the filing is usually completed within a few working days. The timing depends on the MCA portal and the completeness of the documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is filing mandatory when a partner leaves?

Yes. Any change in partners must be reported to the MCA. We confirm the exact form and due date for your change.

What happens if the change is filed late?

Late filing attracts additional fees, and the LLP records remain inaccurate until the filing is made. Please contact us as soon as possible.

Do I need a new LLP agreement?

Often, yes. If the partners or their contributions change, the LLP agreement should be updated to match.

Can a partner leave without the others agreeing?

That depends on the LLP agreement and the law. We review the agreement and explain the position before any filing.

Can this be done from anywhere in India?

Yes. The filing is online through the MCA portal, and we are based in Hapur, Uttar Pradesh.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the change and the filings required. We give a clear quote before we start.
