GST NOTICES

GST Notice Reply and Appeal Handling

Agarwal Mayank & Company — Chartered Accountants Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, India

A GST notice is a formal request for information or a demand for payment. Some notices ask for a document. Others are show cause notices that can lead to a tax demand, penalty or interest. A reply within the deadline is essential, because a missed deadline can result in an order being passed against you without a hearing.

Common Types of GST Notices

  • Mismatch notices: differences between your returns, invoices or GSTR-2B and the department's records
  • Show cause notices: proposed demand for tax, interest or penalty, with a deadline to reply
  • Information requests: notices asking for records, invoices or explanations
  • Registration notices: queries on your registration, or proposed cancellation

Act quickly: Note the date on the notice and the reply deadline. Do not ignore it, even if you believe the notice is wrong. We can often request more time, but the request must be made before the deadline.

Our Approach

Step 1: Review the notice. We read the notice, the period it covers and the amount claimed, and identify what the department is asking for.

Step 2: Reconcile the figures. We check your records against the department's data and separate valid issues from errors.

Step 3: Prepare the reply. We draft a reply with supporting documents, and share it with you for approval before filing.

Step 4: File and follow up. We file the reply on the GST portal and track the department's response. If a personal hearing is fixed, we attend on your behalf where authorised.

Step 5: Order and appeal. If an order is passed that you disagree with, we review the grounds for appeal and the time limit to file it.

Documents We Usually Need

  • A copy of the notice and any attachments
  • Sales and purchase registers for the period covered
  • Relevant invoices, credit notes and payment records
  • Previous returns and reconciliation statements, if available

Timeline

Reply deadlines are set in each notice. The time taken after filing depends on the department, and whether a hearing is needed. We confirm the deadline on the first day we receive the notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

I got a GST notice. What should I do first?

Send us a copy of the notice straight away, with the deadline visible. Do not reply to the department without reviewing it first.

Can a notice be wrong?

Yes, notices sometimes arise from data errors, duplicate entries or wrong GSTINs. We check this before replying.

What if I miss the deadline?

Contact us immediately. Depending on the notice, a request for extension or a rectification route may still be available.

Do you appear before GST authorities?

Where the law allows and the required authorisation is in place, we represent clients at hearings.

Can this be done from anywhere in India?

Yes. Most replies are filed online, and we are based in Hapur, Uttar Pradesh.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the notice, the amount involved and the work required. We give a clear quote after reviewing the notice.
