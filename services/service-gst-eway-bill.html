E-WAY BILL

E-Way Bill Generation and Management

Agarwal Mayank & Company — Chartered Accountants Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, India

An e-way bill is an electronic document that accompanies the movement of goods worth more than the prescribed value, whether between states or within a state, where the rules require it. Moving goods without a valid e-way bill can lead to detention of goods and penalties, so it is important to get this right for every consignment.

When an E-Way Bill Is Required

  • Movement of goods worth more than the prescribed value in a consignment
  • Inter-state movement of goods, subject to the notified exemptions
  • Movement of goods on behalf of a supplier, including transport by a registered transporter

Check the rules: The value limit, the states where intra-state e-way bills apply, and the list of exempt goods change through notifications. We confirm the position for your goods before each dispatch.

What We Manage

  • Generation: Part A and Part B of the e-way bill, with the correct HSN, value and transport details
  • Updates: updating the vehicle number or transporter when the goods are transferred
  • Cancellation: cancelling an e-way bill within the permitted time, where the goods are not moved
  • Extension: extending validity when the journey takes longer than planned
  • Monthly review: checking that e-way bills match your invoices and returns

Our Process

Step 1: Set up. We register your business on the e-way bill portal and link your GSTIN and transporters.

Step 2: Dispatch planning. We confirm, for each consignment, whether a bill is required and generate it before the goods move.

Step 3: Monitoring. We track validity and updates, so goods are not stopped in transit.

Step 4: Reconciliation. We match e-way bills with your sales records each month.

Documents We Need

  • Your GSTIN and login details for the e-way bill portal
  • Invoice details for each consignment, including HSN code and value
  • Transporter ID or vehicle number for the movement
  • Details of the consignee and the delivery location

Frequently Asked Questions

Who generates the e-way bill?

Usually the supplier or the recipient, depending on who arranges the transport. We confirm the responsibility for your transactions.

What happens if goods move without an e-way bill?

The goods can be detained, and a penalty may apply. Keep the bill ready before the vehicle leaves.

Can an e-way bill be cancelled?

Yes, within the time permitted and only if the goods have not been moved. We handle the cancellation.

Can I extend an e-way bill?

Yes, where the journey takes longer than the validity period, the bill can be extended within the allowed time.

Can this be done from anywhere in India?

Yes. The e-way bill portal is online, and we are based in Hapur, Uttar Pradesh.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the number of consignments and whether you need monthly management. We give a clear quote before we start.
