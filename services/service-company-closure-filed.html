COMPANY CLOSURE

Closure of a Pvt Ltd Company That Has Filed All Forms

Agarwal Mayank & Company — Chartered Accountants Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, India

When a private limited company has filed all its statutory returns and financial statements, and the business has stopped, the company can apply to be struck off the register. This is a cleaner option than keeping a dormant company alive, because annual compliance costs continue for as long as the company exists.

When This Service Applies

A company can usually apply for voluntary strike-off under Section 248 of the Companies Act, 2013, where it has not carried on business for the last two years and has no liabilities. Typical conditions are:

  • All annual returns and financial statements are filed and up to date
  • The company has stopped business activity
  • No loans, creditor dues, tax liabilities or pending legal cases remain
  • The directors and shareholders agree to the closure

Important: If the company is still carrying on business or has liabilities, a different route applies, such as a formal winding up. We confirm the right route during the eligibility check.

Our Process, Step by Step

Step 1: Eligibility check. We review the filing history and the company's current position.

Step 2: Liabilities clearance. We confirm there are no dues to creditors, employees, banks or government.

Step 3: Approvals. We prepare the board resolution and shareholder consents required.

Step 4: Application. We file the strike-off application with the ROC through the MCA portal, with the required declarations.

Step 5: ROC notice and objection period. The ROC publishes a notice and allows time for objections. We monitor the portal and respond to queries.

Step 6: Removal. If no valid objection is raised, the company is removed from the register. We provide the final documents.

Documents Usually Required

  • Certificate of incorporation, PAN and TAN of the company
  • Copies of all filed annual returns and financial statements
  • Digital signature certificates (DSC) of the directors
  • Bank statements and confirmation of account closure
  • Details of any GST, income tax or other registrations to be cancelled

Timeline

The application usually takes several months from filing to removal, depending on the ROC's processing and the objection period. We give a realistic timeline after the eligibility check.

Common Reasons Closures Get Delayed

  • Unresolved liabilities or loans still shown in the records
  • Bank accounts still open with transactions
  • Active GST or other registrations that need cancellation
  • Disagreement between directors or shareholders

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a company with filed returns be closed directly?

Yes, if it meets the conditions, including no liabilities and no business for the required period. The eligibility check confirms this.

Do I need to close GST registration first?

GST registration is separate and must be cancelled with the GST department. We can handle it as part of the same engagement.

What if the company still has a bank account?

The account should be closed or its position confirmed before the application. We guide you through what the bank needs.

Will directors be liable after closure?

Directors remain responsible for accurate declarations. We check the position carefully before filing.
