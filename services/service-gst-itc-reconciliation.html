Input tax credit (ITC) is the tax you pay on purchases, which you can set off against your own tax liability. ITC is available only when your suppliers have reported the invoice in their returns, which appears in your GSTR-2B. Every invoice that is missing or mismatched is credit you may lose, so reconciliation each month protects your cash flow.

Why Mismatches Happen

  • Supplier has not filed their return, or filed it late
  • Wrong GSTIN, invoice number or invoice date entered by the supplier or by you
  • Invoice value or tax rate differs between your books and the supplier's return
  • Invoices recorded in a different month or year in your books

Important: Some credits are blocked by law, including certain motor vehicle and personal-use purchases. Credit taken on invoices not reflected in GSTR-2B may also be questioned. We check the eligibility of every item before claiming it.

Our Reconciliation Process

Step 1: Data match. We match your purchase register against GSTR-2B for each period.

Step 2: Classify differences. Each difference is marked as a timing difference, a supplier error, a data error or a blocked credit.

Step 3: Follow up. We prepare a list for your suppliers to correct or file, with the invoice details.

Step 4: Correct and reverse. Where credit must be reversed, we calculate the amount and record it in the return.

Documents We Need

  • Purchase register for the period
  • Supplier invoices and debit or credit notes
  • GSTR-2B for each month, downloaded from the GST portal
  • Details of any blocked or personal-use purchases

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GSTR-2B?

It is an auto-generated statement showing the purchases your suppliers have reported. ITC is generally claimed based on this statement.

If my supplier has not filed, do I lose the credit?

The credit may not be available until the supplier reports the invoice. We track these invoices and claim them once they appear.

Can I claim credit for invoices not in GSTR-2B?

Generally, no. The rules restrict ITC to invoices that appear in GSTR-2B, subject to exceptions. We check each case.

How often should reconciliation be done?

Monthly, before filing the return. Quarterly reconciliation is possible but increases the risk of missed credit.

Can this be done from anywhere in India?

Yes. The work is done online using your purchase records, and we are based in Hapur, Uttar Pradesh.
