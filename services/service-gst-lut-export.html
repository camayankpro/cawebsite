LUT Filing for Exporters Under GST

Agarwal Mayank & Company — Chartered Accountants Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, India

A Letter of Undertaking (LUT) allows a registered exporter to export goods or services without paying integrated tax (IGST), on the condition that the exporter follows the export rules. Without a valid LUT, you must pay IGST upfront and claim a refund later, which blocks working capital.

Who Needs an LUT

  • Exporters of goods, who want to export without paying IGST
  • Exporters of services, including IT and consulting businesses
  • Businesses whose exports are zero-rated and who need to avoid tying up tax money

Renewal: The LUT is generally filed for each financial year, before the first export in that year. Missing the renewal means IGST must be paid on exports until a fresh LUT is filed.

Refund of IGST Paid on Exports

If IGST was paid on an export, the refund is claimed through the GST refund process, within the time limit. Claims must match the shipping bills and the export invoices. We prepare and file these claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an LUT every year?

Generally yes. The LUT is filed for each financial year, so it must be renewed annually. We track the renewal for you.

What if I exported without an LUT?

You may have paid IGST on the export. That tax can be claimed back as a refund, subject to the rules and time limits.

Does the LUT apply to service exports?

Yes. Service exporters can also use an LUT, provided the conditions for export of services are met.

Can I export without an LUT?

Yes, but IGST must be paid on the export, and you claim a refund afterwards. The LUT avoids this upfront payment.
